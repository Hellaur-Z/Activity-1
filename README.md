# Activity-1
Through making a coffee shop poster, I learned the importance of creativity, visual organization, and effective communication in graphic design. I gained skills in choosing appropriate colors, fonts, images, and layouts to create an attractive poster that captures people's attention while clearly presenting the coffee shop's products and identity. One challenge I faced was deciding how to arrange the elements without making the poster look too crowded. I solved this by experimenting with different layouts, adjusting the sizes and positions of the text and images, and choosing colors that complemented each other. In the future, I can apply these skills to create more professional and visually appealing designs while improving my creativity, attention to detail, and knowledge of graphic design tools.
